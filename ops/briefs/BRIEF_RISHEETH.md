## The five questions you must answer cold

1. **"Why is SIFT/AKAZE better than your pipeline on pair_01?"** — Zero sun difference, same frame:
   the one case where classical detectors are strongest and illumination normalisation buys
   nothing. Our advantage appears with a sun difference; the synthetic sweep is where that is
   measured.
2. **"What is Lowe's ratio and why 0.75?"** — Nearest/second-nearest descriptor distance; 0.75 is
   the standard default. Know it.
3. **"Did your baselines get the same preprocessing?"** — No: raw DN percentile-stretched, no
   illumination normalisation, matches unfiltered. That is deliberate — it isolates the matcher.
   The ablation with normalisation is the missing row (Config 2).
4. **"Are your Tier D numbers accuracies?"** — No. Fit residuals on wrong matches against a
   wrongly-lit render. Say it before anyone else does.
5. **"Why AKAZE and not ORB?"** — AKAZE's nonlinear scale space preserves edges; ORB's binary
   descriptor is fastest but least accurate here (24.4 px on pair_01).
