Lunar registration that knows when it is wrong

We align Chandrayaan-2 images to lunar references across Sun angle, scale and sensor. Then a second test, which never reads the matches, checks the result region by region and says whether it can be trusted.

Across 160 real ground windows and 8 instrument pairings, the check raised no false alarm in either Sun population, and caught every planted 5 m error on the windows whose Suns were within 10° of each other.

This page reads the evidence logs, whose rows were measured at the freeze commit , the same commit the submitted deck quotes. The per-pair panels come from the pipeline's own saved run of each pair, which can differ from the logged row by a few inliers because MAGSAC++ samples at random; the verdicts do not. Nothing here is drawn by hand or filled with example data.

Try a pair of your own

Runs core/pipeline.py, the same function that produced every frozen number, on this computer.

Choose two images

GeoTIFF, PDS (.img, .xml, .lbl), PNG and JPEG are accepted. Expect up to a minute on a busy laptop. Nothing is saved: the uploads sit in a temporary folder that is deleted when the run ends, and no evidence log is touched. If the matcher fails on your pair, the check should say so, and that is a pass.

Every pairing, and its verdict

From evaluation/real_pairs_log.csv and the pipeline's saved run of each pair. One representative of every instrument pairing, and of all three verdicts.

    A square is verified only with at least 3 inliers, a local inlier ratio of 0.5 or more, and a cross-correlation of the warped image against the reference that peaks within 2 px at 0.3 or more. A square with no matches is left empty rather than filled in from its neighbours. The frame is refused when fewer than a quarter of the squares the check could measure agree with the matcher.

    The whole overlap, not chosen windows

    From REPORT.md, The whole lit overlap of one OHRC frame with one NAC.

    Every non-overlapping 640-pixel window the cutter finds in shared, lit, textured ground of one OHRC frame and NAC M1153871873LE, chosen before any matching ran: 37 windows, 13.1 km², all 37 accepted. The held-out median is 0.61 px, which is 0.57 m on the 0.93 m NAC grid.

    36 of the 37 are under 3 px. The one that is not reports 316 px at an inlier ratio of 0.542: the held-out median is only robust while the inlier ratio stays well above one half. The image evidence says that window is registered; it is marked, not averaged away.

    Wrong answers, planted on purpose

    From evaluation/trust_real_calibration.csv. 30 real windows in two Sun populations, never pooled.

    We move the true answer by a known distance and plant matches that agree with the wrong answer perfectly, so nothing the matcher reports can give it away. Then we ask the check. At 0 m it must stay silent, and it does: 0 of 44 and 0 of 16. From 5 m it flags every planted error on the near-Sun windows. Below 2 m it catches almost nothing, and that is the honest floor of the method.

    A rotation or scale change about the centre leaves the centre still and moves the corners, so a verdict for the whole frame watches the wrong place: at 3 m of corner movement it refuses 0 of 120 trials, at 5 m only 15. The squares carry it instead.

    How it works

    core/pipeline.py, in the order it runs.

    1. Input pairA Chandrayaan-2 image and a lunar reference
    2. One ground scaleBoth resampled to one ground distance per pixel
    3. Lighting removedReduced to gradient orientation
    4. LoFTRDense matching without detectors, tiled, on the CPU
    5. Sub-pixelEach match refined by correlation
    6. MAGSAC++Outliers rejected, the transform fitted
    7. CoverageHow the inliers spread over the 8×8 grid
    The independent area check

    Cross-correlates the warped image against the reference, square by square, and never reads a match position. The squares vote on the matcher's transform, and this is the only step allowed to overrule the others.

    AgreesThe aligned GeoTIFF on the reference grid, control points for GDAL and QGIS, an ISIS match list, the metrics and the trust map.
    UnconfirmedKept and labelled, not certified.
    ContradictedThe matcher's answer is refused. A phase-correlation fallback is delivered, declared as a fallback, with its uncertainty in metres.

    Runs on a laptop CPU with no discrete GPU and the network off: a median of 8.2 s per 640-pixel window over 89 OHRC to NAC windows.

    Words we hold to

    Cross-sensor means different instruments. NAC with NAC, and TMC-2 fore with aft, are the same sensor and are labelled so.

    Multi-modal means visible with infrared, or optical with elevation. Two panchromatic cameras are not.

    Sub-pixel always names its grid and gives metres.
