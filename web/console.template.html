Same ground, two Suns

At the lunar south pole the Sun never climbs high, so shadows run long and swing right round the compass. Every shadow edge a matcher could use moves, or turns inside out.

Sun A is the OHRC frame's own, 7° above the horizon. Drag Sun B round the compass. Relief and shadows from the LOLA 60 m elevation model; contours every 250 m.

Every pairing, and what the check said

One window from every instrument pairing we tested, and all three verdicts. Pick a plate. Hatching in magenta marks what the system refused.

Squares shown clear are verified: matches and pixels agree. Hatched squares are weak. Dotted squares had no matches, so nothing is claimed there. A square is verified only with at least 3 inliers, a local inlier ratio of 0.5 or more, and a correlation that peaks within 2 px at 0.3 or more.

The check, tested on purpose

A failure detector is only worth something if its false alarms and its blind spot are measured. So we planted wrong answers of known size in 30 real windows and asked it.

0 of 44false alarms with the Suns within 10°, and 0 of 16 with them 132° to 174° apart

5 mand up: every planted error is flagged on the near-Sun windows. Below 2 m almost nothing is, and that is the floor.

A rotation or scale change about the centre leaves the centre still and moves the corners, so a verdict for the whole frame watches the wrong place: at 3 m of corner movement it refuses 0 of 120 trials, at 5 m only 15. The squares carry it instead.

Every non-overlapping 640-pixel window in the shared, lit, textured ground of one OHRC frame and NAC M1153871873LE, chosen before any matching ran.

How it runs

core/pipeline.py, on a laptop CPU with no discrete GPU and the network off: a median of 8.2 s per 640-pixel window over 89 OHRC to NAC windows.

Both images go to one ground scale. Lighting is reduced to gradient orientation. LoFTR matches them densely, tiled, on the CPU; each match is refined to sub-pixel by correlation; MAGSAC++ rejects outliers and fits the transform; the inliers' spread over the 8×8 grid is measured. Then the independent area check cross-correlates the warped image against the reference, square by square, and never reads a match position. It is the only step allowed to overrule the others. If it agrees, the result ships as a GeoTIFF on the reference grid with control points for GDAL and QGIS, an ISIS match list, the metrics and the trust map. If it is unconfirmed, it is kept and labelled. If it contradicts, the matcher's answer is refused and a phase-correlation fallback is delivered, declared as such, with its uncertainty in metres.

Words we hold to

Cross-sensor means different instruments. NAC with NAC, and TMC-2 fore with aft, are the same sensor and are labelled so.

Multi-modal means visible with infrared, or optical with elevation. Two panchromatic cameras are not.

Sub-pixel always names its grid and gives metres.

Reproduce every number

This page reads the evidence logs, whose rows were measured at the freeze commit , the commit the submitted deck quotes. The per-pair plates come from the pipeline's saved run of each pair, which can differ from the logged row by a few inliers because MAGSAC++ samples at random; the verdicts do not.

python -m ops.freeze re-runs every piece of evidence on one clean commit; python -m ops.make_report renders REPORT.md from the logs.
