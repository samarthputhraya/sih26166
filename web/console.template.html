Lunar registration that knows when it is wrong

Chandrayaan-2 images aligned to lunar references across Sun angle, scale and sensor, with a verdict for every region of every result. The verdict comes from an independent area check that never sees the matches. Everything below is measured data from the evidence logs, not a mock-up.

TRUST

One it accepts, one it refuses

evaluation/real_pairs_log.csv · cached run_all() output
Blink comparator

Blink between the reference and the registered image, as astronomers do to find what moved. On the accepted pair nothing jumps, although the two Suns are 174° apart and every shadow flips. On the refused pair the system has already told you not to trust it.

verified: matches and pixels agree weak: measured, does not hold no evidence: unmeasured, never guessed

A cell is verified only with at least 3 inliers, a local inlier ratio of 0.5 or more, and an FFT cross-correlation of the warped image against the reference that peaks within 2 px at NCC 0.3 or more. A cell with no matches is no evidence. It is never painted green by interpolation. The frame is contradicted when fewer than 25% of measurable cells agree with the matcher's transform, and then nothing in it is verified.

TILE

The whole overlap, not chosen windows

REPORT.md · The whole lit overlap of one OHRC frame with one NAC

Window footprints in their true map positions (Moon 2015 south polar stereographic, metres). No two footprints overlap (they touch only at corners), so the 13.1 km² is distinct ground. Deeper fill is a lower held-out median. The dashed window is the one the text beside it explains.

Every non-overlapping 640-px window the cutter finds in shared, lit, textured ground of one OHRC frame and NAC M1153871873LE, selected before any matching ran: 37 windows, 13.1 km², 37 of 37 accepted. Held-out median 0.61 px = 0.57 m on the 0.93 m NAC grid. A held-out residual is not ground truth.

36 of 37 are under 3 px. One reports 316 px, at an inlier ratio of 0.542. The held-out median is the median of the 20% of matches the fit never saw, and it is only robust while the inlier ratio stays well above 0.5. Independent image evidence says that window is registered: the exported product correlates with its reference at NCC +0.87 to +0.95 across all 37, and under the declared transform the median error over all matches on the worst window is 1.08 px. It is a limit of the metric, stated here rather than averaged away.

CAL

Planted wrong answers

evaluation/trust_real_calibration.csv
22 windows, Sun azimuths under 10° apart 8 SAC windows, 132° to 174° apart

We shift the true transform by a known distance and plant matches that agree with the wrong answer perfectly, so nothing the matcher reports can reveal it. Then we ask the area check. At 0 m it must stay silent: 0 of 44 and 0 of 16 false alarms. From 5 m it flags every one on the near-Sun windows. Below 3 m it sees almost nothing, and that floor is the honest limit of the method.

Rotation and scale are caught cell by cell, not by the frame verdict. A rotation about the centre leaves the centre still and moves the corners, so the frame can still say agrees while a corner is 3 px out. The 8×8 map is what carries it. The second meter is the control: the map is not simply refusing everything.

FLOW

The pipeline, in the order it runs

core/pipeline.py · run_all()
  1. Input pairChandrayaan-2 optical + a lunar reference
  2. Common scaleboth resampled to one ground sample distance
  3. Illuminationreduced to gradient orientation, so lighting drops out
  4. LoFTRdense, detector-free matching, tiled, on CPU
  5. Sub-pixelNCC refinement of every match
  6. MAGSAC++outlier rejection and the transform
  7. Distribution8×8 coverage and spread of the inliers
Independent area checkcross-correlates the warped image against the reference, cell by cell. It never uses match positions. The cells vote on the matcher's transform.
AgreesAligned GeoTIFF on the reference grid, GDAL and QGIS control points, an ISIS-style match list, metrics and the trust map.
UnconfirmedKept and labelled. Not certified.
ContradictedMatcher output refused. Phase-correlation fallback, declared as a fallback, with its uncertainty in metres.

CPU only, no discrete GPU, fully offline. Median 8.2 s per 640-px window over 89 OHRC-to-NAC windows.

Words we hold to

Cross-sensor means different instruments. NAC ↔ NAC and TMC-2 fore ↔ aft are the same sensor and are labelled so.

Multi-modal means visible ↔ infrared or elevation. Two panchromatic cameras are not.

Sub-pixel always names its grid and gives metres.
