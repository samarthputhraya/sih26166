Day 6 Part 1 - Ratio Picture Experiment

Original false-positive baseline:
Threshold 30: 62.0%
Threshold 60: 41.5%
Threshold 90: 31.0%
Threshold 120: 20.4%

Ratio-picture result:
Threshold 30: 43.24%
Threshold 60: 19.58%
Threshold 90: 10.77%
Threshold 120: 5.79%

Observation:
The pixel-wise ratio approach reduced the falsely flagged area at every tested threshold. The strongest result was at threshold 120, where the flagged area decreased from 20.4% to 5.79%. The ratio approach therefore shows useful improvement over the original absolute-difference baseline.
